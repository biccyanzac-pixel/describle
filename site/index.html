          How it works & credits

          Your description is turned into a vector by a small language model, all-MiniLM-L6-v2 (Apache-2.0), running on ONNX Runtime Web (MIT). It's compared with the definitions of 15,000 common words from WordNet 3.0 (© Princeton University, WordNet 3.0 licence), and the closest word is the guess.

          Word frequencies: wordfreq data (CC BY-SA 4.0); the word list derived from it (vocab-v1.json) is shared under CC BY-SA 4.0. Blocklist: LDNOOBW (CC BY 4.0). Stemming: stemmer (MIT). Tokenizer: @huggingface/tokenizers (Apache-2.0).
